BEYOND THE BRIEF · UPV IDEAS #1, #3, #4

From explorer to incident explorer

#3 Traces

An optional trace id groups the events of one IE, flow or sensor.

#4 Incident timeline and alerts

Events in order, each verified against its own block id; alerts on integrity failures or critical tags.

#1 MQTT

Every record and alert on a live broker feed, plus a second delivery path.

UPV challenge slides p.6: “The jury will bear in consideration the level of complexity of the final product.”

The UPV slides list four ideas of growing complexity. We built the explorer UI and went on to three of the extras. Traces group related events, for example everything about one infrastructure element. The incident timeline puts them in order and verifies each one against its own block id. Alerts fire on integrity failures or on critical tags such as security alerts. And MQTT gives a live feed plus a second delivery path.
